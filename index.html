My favorite Call of Duty Modern Warfare Missions

The Modern Warfare trilogy is known for its intense campaigns, memorable characters, and iconic missions. Here are three of my favorite missions, one from each game.

Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare - All Ghillied Up

All Ghillied Up is one of the most memorable missions in the original Modern Warfare, focusing on stealth and taking place in Pripyat.

Modern Warfare 2 - No russian

No Russian is one of the most controversial and unforgettable missions in the Modern Warfare series, playing a major role in the game's story.

Modern Warfare 3 - Return to Sender

Return to Sender is my favorite mission from Modern Warfare 3, featuring an intense assault and some of the most memorable action in the campaign.
